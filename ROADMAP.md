# The Bridge: tools roadmap

Helper tools that support Alexander's move into UX/UI design, added **one at a time**,
each only after its **unlock** is reached. Dates are targets; unlocks are what matter.

**Rules**
- Only one new tool in progress at a time. Finish it before the next.
- Building a tool is a Mon-Wed task ("Ask Claude to set up X"). Never on a shift day.
- Prefer adding to Bridge Bot or a scheduled task over making a new bot.
- Tools draft and suggest. Alexander posts, sends and applies himself.

| # | Tool | What it does | Unlocks when | Build size |
|---|------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| 1 | **Survey response watcher** | Daily check of the Houseplant Care Survey. When someone leaves contact details, pings Alexander and drafts a friendly invite for a 15-min chat. | Survey recruiting post is live AND the first response comes in | Scheduled task |
| 2 | **Case study review buddy** | Checks each finished Fernly case study section against what junior UX hiring managers look for. | Alexander starts writing up the Fernly case study | None (ask Claude with "Claude:" or in chat) |
| 3 | **Learning-in-public post drafter** | Every Monday, turns the weekly recap into a short LinkedIn post draft. Never posts by itself. | Fernly case study is published (target 30 Nov 2026) | Bridge Bot add-on |
| 4 | **Job & opportunity scout** | Weekly top 3 junior UX/UI roles, apprenticeships, internships and portfolio reviews (incl. internal Walmart UX roles), with links. | Portfolio has Fernly + one more finished case study | Scheduled task |
| 5 | **Interview practice partner** | Mock UX interviews: "walk me through your case study", whiteboard prompts, feedback. | First job application sent | None (chat with Claude) |
| 6 | **Template buyer helper** | Agent that answers buyer questions about the Figma portfolio template (maybe Claude Managed Agents). | Template is selling | Bigger build, check cost first |

**Parked on purpose:** Claude Duet (revisit after Fernly ships; would replace the 6:30am "Claude:" check,
not Bridge Bot). "Daily UX tip" style bots (noise, no portfolio progress).
