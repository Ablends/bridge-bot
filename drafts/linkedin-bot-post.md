**I finished something. 🎉**

I'm a UX/UI design student, and I have a habit of starting projects and not finishing them. So I built a tool to help me finish things.

**Orbital Bot** is a Slack bot that keeps me accountable:
☀️ Every morning it sends me ONE task, tiny on my work days and bigger on my free days
✅ One tap to check in when it's done, no typing
📦 Every Monday it posts a recap of what I got done

🎥 The video is a quick demo: I ask for my task, it sends exactly one thing to do, and a single tap checks it off. That one tap was the whole point. If checking in takes effort, I won't do it.

I'm new to programming, so I built it with Claude (an AI assistant) writing the code while I made the decisions: what it should do, how it should feel, and what to leave out. It turned out to be a design project as much as a coding one. The hardest part was keeping it simple.

It's live now, and its first job is getting me through my Google UX Design course.

Next up: my first portfolio case study. 🌿
